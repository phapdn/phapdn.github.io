I received the Ph.D. degree in Information and Communication Engineering (Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering) from Inha University, South Korea, in 2022. Subsequently, I held a post-doctoral fellowship at the Artificial Intelligence System-on-Chip (AI-SoC) Research Center, Inha University, from September 2022 to January 2024.

Currently, I am a lecturer/researcher in Vietnam-Korea University of Information and Communication Technology (VKU), a member of The University of Danang (UDN), located in Da Nang City, 550000, Vietnam. 

## Research Interests

My primary research interests include algorithms and architectures for:

* Post-Quantum Cryptography
* Homomorphic Encryption based PPML
* Cybersecurity
* Hardware Accelerator Architecture
* Tiny Machine Learning
